# Practice Areas

Harbor Legal (fictional demo firm) handles the following areas. This
document is a general guide for intake, not legal advice.

## Residential real estate

Purchase and sale contracts, title review, and closings for residential
property. We do not currently handle commercial real estate transactions.

## Estate planning

Wills, revocable trusts, and powers of attorney for individuals and
couples. We do not handle probate litigation or contested estates —
those are referred out.

## Small business formation

LLC and corporation formation, operating agreements, and basic contract
review for businesses with fewer than 20 employees. We do not handle
securities offerings or venture financing.

## What we don't handle

Criminal defense, family law (divorce/custody), personal injury, and
immigration are outside our practice areas. If your matter falls into one
of these, we can suggest where to look, but we won't be able to take the
case.
