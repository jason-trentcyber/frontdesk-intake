# Consultation Policy

Harbor Legal (fictional demo firm) offers a free 20-minute initial phone
consultation for new matters within our practice areas. This is intended
to confirm we're a fit and to discuss scope, not to give legal advice on
the call itself.

## Scheduling

Initial consultations are scheduled Tuesday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to
4:00 PM. Same-week scheduling is usually available; urgent matters
(closing deadlines within 10 business days) are flagged for priority
scheduling.

## What to bring or send ahead

For real estate matters: the purchase contract, if one exists. For estate
planning: a rough list of assets and who you'd want to inherit them. For
business formation: a one-paragraph description of what the business
does and who the owners are.

## After the consultation

If we're a fit, we'll send an engagement letter with our fee structure
for your specific matter before any billable work begins.
