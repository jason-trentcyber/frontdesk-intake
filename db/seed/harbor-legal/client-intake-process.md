# Client Intake Process

How a new matter moves through Harbor Legal (fictional demo firm) from
first contact to engagement.

## Step 1: Initial inquiry

Submitted through the contact form or by phone. We aim to respond within
one business day with either a consultation time or a referral if the
matter is outside our practice areas.

## Step 2: Conflict check

Before scheduling a consultation, we run a conflict-of-interest check
against current and former clients. This is why we ask for the names of
all parties involved in your matter, not just your own.

## Step 3: Consultation

A free 20-minute call (see our consultation policy document) to confirm
fit and discuss scope.

## Step 4: Engagement letter

If we proceed, you'll receive an engagement letter describing the scope
of work, fee structure, and a retainer amount if applicable. No billable
work begins until the engagement letter is signed and, for hourly
matters, the retainer is received.
