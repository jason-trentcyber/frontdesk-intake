# Fee Schedule

General guidance for Harbor Legal (fictional demo firm). Actual fees are
confirmed in your engagement letter and can vary by matter complexity.

## Flat-fee matters

- Simple will (single person): $450
- Simple will (couple, mirror wills): $750
- Revocable trust package (individual): $1,800
- LLC formation (single-member, one state): $900

## Hourly matters

Residential real estate transactions and small business contract review
are typically billed hourly at $275/hour, with an estimated range given
before work begins. Most home purchase closings run 4–8 hours of attorney
time.

## Retainers

Hourly matters require a retainer, collected before work begins and
billed against as work is done. Any unused retainer balance is refunded
at the close of the matter.
