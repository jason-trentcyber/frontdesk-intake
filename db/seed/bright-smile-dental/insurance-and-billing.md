# Insurance and Billing

Bright Smile Dental (fictional demo practice) is in-network with the
following plans: Delta Dental PPO, Cigna Dental, MetLife, and Guardian.
We are out-of-network with Aetna DMO and most HMO plans — ask at check-in
if you're not sure which plan you have.

## What we collect at the visit

We collect your estimated co-pay or coinsurance at check-in, based on a
benefits estimate from your insurer. This is an estimate, not a
guarantee of coverage; the final amount is set once your insurer
processes the claim.

## Payment plans

For treatment plans over $500, we offer an interest-free payment plan
over 3, 6, or 12 months through our billing coordinator. Ask at your
consultation.

## Without insurance

Patients without dental insurance receive a 15% courtesy discount on
most procedures when payment is made in full at the time of service.
