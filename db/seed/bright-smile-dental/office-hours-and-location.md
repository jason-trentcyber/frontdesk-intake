# Office Hours and Location

Bright Smile Dental (fictional demo practice) is open:

- Monday–Thursday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Friday: 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: closed

We are located at 42 Maple Grove Lane, Suite 100, Springfield. Parking is
available in the lot behind the building; the entrance is wheelchair
accessible from the rear lot.

## Holidays

The office is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day,
Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and Christmas Day. If a
holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, we are also closed the nearest
weekday.

## Emergencies

For a dental emergency outside office hours (a knocked-out tooth,
uncontrolled bleeding, or severe swelling), call the office line — the
recorded message gives the on-call dentist's number. For anything
life-threatening, call 911 first.
