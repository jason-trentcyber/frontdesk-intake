# Common Procedures

A quick reference for questions about the most common things we do at
Bright Smile Dental (fictional demo practice).

## Cleanings

A routine cleaning (prophylaxis) is recommended every six months for most
patients. It includes scaling, polishing, and a fluoride treatment on
request. Most insurance plans cover two cleanings per calendar year at
100%.

## Fillings

We use tooth-colored composite fillings for cavities in both front and
back teeth. A single-surface filling typically takes 20–30 minutes under
local anesthetic; you can eat normally once the numbness wears off,
usually 2–3 hours.

## Crowns

A crown usually takes two visits: the first to prepare the tooth and take
an impression (you'll wear a temporary crown for 1–2 weeks), the second
to place the permanent crown. Same-day crowns are not currently offered
at this location.

## Whitening

In-office whitening takes about an hour and lightens most teeth several
shades in one visit. Take-home tray kits are also available and are
typically used nightly for 1–2 weeks.
