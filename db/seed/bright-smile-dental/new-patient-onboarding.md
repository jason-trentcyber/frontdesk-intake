# New Patient Onboarding

Welcome to Bright Smile Dental (fictional demo practice). Here's what to
expect for your first visit.

## Before you arrive

Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete new patient paperwork, or fill
it out ahead of time using the link in your confirmation email. Bring a
photo ID and your insurance card, if you have one.

## What the first visit includes

A first visit is typically a full exam, a set of diagnostic X-rays if you
haven't had any in the last 12 months, a cleaning, and a conversation
with the dentist about any concerns or treatment needed. Plan for about
60–75 minutes.

## Children

We see patients starting at age 1 (or the eruption of the first tooth).
A parent or guardian must stay in the building for patients under 12.

## Transferring records

If you're transferring from another practice, ask your previous dentist's
office to send your records and any recent X-rays — we're happy to
request them on your behalf if you give us their contact information.
